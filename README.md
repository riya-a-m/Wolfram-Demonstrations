## Introduction
This project explores probability and statistics through interactive simulations using the Wolfram Demonstrations Project. The demonstrations help understand important mathematical concepts through visual representations and practical observations.

## Demonstrations

### 1. Probability of Being Sick After Having Tested Positive for a Disease (Bayes' Rule)
This demonstration explores Bayes' theorem and how the probability of having a disease changes after receiving a positive test result. Different values of sensitivity, specificity, and disease prevalence are examined.

[View Demonstration](https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/ProbabilityOfBeingSickAfterHavingTestedPositiveForADiseaseBa/)

### 2. Mean, Median and Quartiles in Skewed Distributions
This demonstration explores the relationship between mean, median, and quartiles in skewed distributions. Different Gamma and Beta distribution parameters are observed to understand how skewness affects these statistical measures.

[View Demonstration](https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/MeanMedianAndQuartilesInSkewedDistributions/)
